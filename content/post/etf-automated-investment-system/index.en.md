<p>When investing in index funds, the greatest challenge is often not stock selection, but rather maintaining discipline and periodically rebalancing assets. Traditional Excel spreadsheets cannot automatically fetch the latest constituent stock prices and dividend information, leading investors to spend a significant amount of effort on manual maintenance.</p>

<h2>1. Why Does a Portfolio Need "Automated Rebalancing Alerts"?</h2>
<p>When the market experiences significant volatility, the weight ratio of stocks and bonds (or high-dividend and market-cap ETFs) will deviate from their original targets. A systematic allocation drift alert mechanism can proactively notify you when assets deviate by more than 5%, helping investors perform objective buy-low, sell-high operations.</p>

<h2>2. Core Tracking Indicators Analysis</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Allocation Drift:</strong> The difference between the current holding ratio and the target ratio.</li>
  <li><strong>Annualized Sharpe Ratio:</strong> Evaluates the excess return obtained for each unit of risk taken.</li>
  <li><strong>Constituent Overlap Analysis:</strong> Avoids simultaneously purchasing multiple ETFs with highly overlapping constituent stocks.</li>
</ul>
