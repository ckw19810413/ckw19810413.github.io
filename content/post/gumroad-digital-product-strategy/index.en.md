## Step 1: Product Selection — Finding Your Goldmine

Most people make their first mistake by deciding "what product I want to make" before validating the market. The right approach is backwards.

### Methodology: Pain-Point-First

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **What pain am I solving?** People pay because they hurt. Not because something is "nice," but because something is "unbearable."
2. **How intense is the pain?** Mild inconvenience doesn't drive sales. Urgent pain does.
3. **Do I already have a solution?** The best products come from your own real needs.

### Four Battle-Tested Product Directions

Based on Slashman Tools' experience, these four directions have exceptional potential in 2026:

| Product Type | Price Range | Dev Difficulty | Market Size | Recommendation |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|-------------|----------------|
| **AI Prompt Library** | $19–$49 | ⭐ Low | Large | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Workflow Template Pack** | $29–$79 | ⭐ Low | Medium-Large | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Online Course** | $49–$299 | ⭐⭐ Medium | Large | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **SaaS Tool** | $9–$99/mo | ⭐⭐⭐ High | Medium | ⭐⭐⭐ |

**AI Prompt Library** is the ideal starting product. The reason is simple: you don't need any coding ability, just a good understanding of how to use AI tools. And the market demand is exploding — everyone is using AI, but few know "how to use it well."

**Workflow Template Packs** are the second high-value direction. Notion templates, Google Sheets templates, Feishu templates — these are things businesses actually need in their daily operations. The key is "integration": don't make one template, make a "system."

### Validate Your Product Topic

Before investing time, validate your topic has real market demand:

**Method 1: Community Poll**

Post a survey on X (Twitter), LinkedIn, or Xiaohongshu:

> "How much time do you spend daily on [some repetitive task]? If a tool could save you 50% of that time, would you pay for it?"

After 50+ responses, if at least 20% say "I'd pay," that's a viable topic.

**Method 2: Competitor Analysis**

Search your product category on Gumroad and observe:
- Price ranges of top 10 products
- Review count (more means bigger demand)
- Common pain points in reviews (your opportunity)
- Design style of product pages

**Method 3: Pre-sale Test**

Before a full launch, publish a "coming soon" page and collect email signups. If 10+ out of 100 visitors leave their email, there's sufficient market demand.

## Step 2: Pricing Strategy — Making Consumers "Can't Resist Buying"

Pricing is the most critical decision for digital products. Price too high → nobody buys. Price too low → nobody trusts it.

### Slashman Tools' Pricing Model

| Product | Price | Strategy | Rationale |
|---------|-------|----------|-----------|
| AI Prompt Library | $29 | Low-barrier entry | Anyone can try; lowers decision threshold |
| Feishu Templates | $49 | Mid-high value | Targets enterprise users, high perceived value |
| AI Course | $99 | High-value delivery | Full course, low time cost but high value perception |
| ETF Dashboard | $29/mo or $199 lifetime | Dual model | Lowers entry barrier while satisfying one-time buyers |

### 5 Psychology Principles of Pricing

**1. Leverage Pricing**
Never sell just one product. Offer three price tiers:

| Tier | Price | Content | Psychological Effect |
|------|-------|---------|---------------------|
| Basic | $19 | Basic version | Anchor price (makes Pro look like a deal) |
| Pro | $29 | Full version ← primary target | Best value perception |
| Premium | $49 | Full + consultation | Makes Pro look even better |

**2. Anchor Effect**
Display an "artificially high" comparison price. E.g., "Was $99, now $29 (70% off limited time)." Even if your product was never $99, this comparison makes $29 feel like a deal.

**3. Psychological Pricing**
$29, not $30. $49, not $50. This isn't superstition — it's a proven fact from decades of data. Consumers read prices "left to right": $29 reads as "twenty-something," not "approaching thirty."

**4. Scarcity**
"Discount until [date]," "First 100 names only" — these aren't cheap tricks, they're genuine psychological drivers. Gumroad's built-in "Limited Offer" feature is your friend.

**5. Subscription vs. One-time Purchase**
For tool-type products (like ETF Dashboard), offering both subscription and one-time purchase is the most effective approach. Subscriptions bring recurring revenue; one-time purchases attract users who don't want to be locked in.

> **Battle-tested insight:** After switching the ETF Dashboard from pure subscription ($29/mo) to "subscription + lifetime license $199" dual model, conversion rate increased 35%.

## Step 3: Product Page Optimization — Turning Visitors into Buyers

Your product page is your 24/7 salesperson. A good page converts 5% of visitors to buyers; a bad page might convert only 0.5%.

### 7 Essential Elements of a Product Page

**1. Headline: One-Second Attention**

> ❌ "AI Prompt Library 2026 Edition"
> ✅ "200+ Precision AI Prompt Templates, 10x Your Work Efficiency"

Characteristics of a good headline:
- Contains numbers (more specific and credible)
- States value (not features, but results)
- Targets pain points (makes readers feel "this is exactly what I'm looking for")

**2. Subheadline: Extending the Headline**

The subheadline should answer "what's in it for me":

> "Copy-paste ready prompt templates covering 20+ work scenarios, from copywriting to coding. Zero learning curve."

**3. Product Image: Visual Impact**

The product cover image determines 80% of click-through rate. A good cover should:
- Use high color contrast
- Show screenshots of actual product content
- Include keywords (for search engine readability)
- Recommended size: 1280x800 pixels

**4. Pain Point Description: Creating Empathy**

Before describing the product, describe the problem. This makes readers feel "you get me":

> "Have you ever experienced:
> - Spent 20 minutes writing a long prompt, but the AI response still missed the mark?
> - Switched to a different AI tool, and previously good prompts completely stopped working?
> - Spending too much time crafting prompts instead of creating actual content?
>
> If you nodded yes, this product is for you."

**5. Product Content List: Making Value Tangible**

Don't say "includes many prompts." Say "includes 200+ carefully crafted prompts, covering these scenarios:"

Then list categorized scenarios. The more specific, the more value readers perceive.

**6. Testimonials: Social Proof**

Even when starting out, you can accumulate reviews by:
- Asking friends and peers to use it free in exchange for feedback
- Inviting early users on social media to leave reviews
- Offering "early bird discount" in exchange for honest reviews

**7. Call to Action (CTA): Reducing Hesitation**

Add CTA after every key paragraph:

> "👉 [Download AI Prompt Library Now](#) — from $29, 7-day no-questions-asked refund guarantee"

### Gumroad Page Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Cover image size ≥ 1280x800 pixels
- [ ] Headline contains keywords and numbers
- [ ] Clear pain point description
- [ ] Product content has specific list
- [ ] At least 3 user reviews
- [ ] Offers refund guarantee
- [ ] Has preview or free sample
- [ ] CTA button stands out

Products are ready, pages are polished — but you need people to see them.

Here are the 6 community marketing channels validated by Slashman Tools:

### 4.1 X (Twitter) — The Best Platform for Tech Products

**Strategy: Build a Content Flywheel**

```
Daily AI tip shares → Attract followers → Product promotion → Sales increase → More time to create content
```

**Concrete tactics:**

- **Share one AI tip daily**: Not ads, genuinely valuable content
- **Promote products once per week**: Intercalated among 6 days of valuable content

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a large following to start selling?**
A: No. Your first customer might come from any channel — a meaningful community reply, a valuable blog post, or even an accidental social media share. Focus on providing value, not accumulating follower numbers.

**Q: What if nobody buys?**
A: Assume first that your product page or promotion has an issue. Check: (1) Does the page clearly state value? (2) Is there enough traffic? (3) Is the pricing reasonable? (4) Is there social proof? Usually changing one factor yields noticeable improvement.

**Q: How much time does it take to maintain?**
A: The ideal state is 2-3 hours per week. Content creation time can be greatly reduced through AI agents. Once the system is built, true passive income is achievable.

**Q: What about tax and compliance?**
A: Gumroad handles most tax compliance (VAT, Sales Tax), but you still need to declare income tax personally. Consult a local accountant, especially for cross-border sales.

**Q: When should I launch a subscription product?**
A: When your product requires continuous updates (like tool software, data services), subscription is the best choice. For one-time-delivery digital products (prompt libraries, templates, courses), one-time purchase is more suitable.
