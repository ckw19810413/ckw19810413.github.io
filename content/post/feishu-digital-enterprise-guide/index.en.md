<p>When modern enterprises and highly efficient teams undergo digital transformation, they often encounter a pain point: introducing a powerful collaboration platform (like Feishu / Lark), but not knowing where to start when faced with a blank workspace. This article will analyze in depth how to use Feishu Bitable and automated workflows to quickly build a digital operations system exclusively for your enterprise.</p>

<h2>1. The Four Core Pillars of a Digital Enterprise</h2>
<p>A complete enterprise digital system needs to cover the following four modules:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Sales and CRM Funnel:</strong> Track customer leads, stage conversion rates, and automated follow-up reminders.</li>
  <li><strong>Project and OKR Tracking:</strong> Break down quarterly goals into Sprint boards and Gantt charts to achieve visualized progress management.</li>
  <li><strong>Administrative and Approval Workflows:</strong> Achieve zero-paper automated approvals for processes like leave, reimbursement, and procurement.</li>
  <li><strong>Financial and Revenue Dashboards:</strong> Real-time statistics on monthly income and expenses, budget execution status, and invoice tracking.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. Why Choose Bitable?</h2>
<p>Compared to traditional Excel, Feishu Bitable features powerful data association, multi-view switching (Kanban, Gantt chart, calendar), and automated trigger functions. As long as the data structure is set up properly, it can increase cross-departmental collaboration efficiency by 300%.</p>

<h2>3. Implementation Steps for Automated Workflows</h2>
<p>It is recommended that teams start by trying out "automatic conversion of meeting minutes to to-do items" and "automatic routing of customer leads." Through simple automation rule settings, manual repetitive operations can be significantly reduced, allowing the team to focus on core business growth.</p>
